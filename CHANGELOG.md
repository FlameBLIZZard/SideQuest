# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-20)

Motion pass across the suite:

- **Shared motion layer**: spring button presses, hover lifts, toasts that bounce in and bump on repeat, page enter and leave transitions between the launcher and apps, staggered sidebars, a smooth theme fade, and a reduced-motion fallback that turns it all off.
- **Launcher**: cards tilt toward the pointer with a moving light, icons wiggle on hover, filtering animates cards into place, and a new "Surprise me" roulette picks a random app. Going back from an app resets the launcher properly.
- **Loft**: steps light up as the playhead hits them and the current chord lifts.
- **Boop**: pads press down and send out a ring when they play.
- **Namewell**: copied names flash and favourite stars spin.
- **Wallsmith**: tiny previews are no longer blurry, and New seed or Shuffle pops the canvas.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

First release with twelve apps and a launcher:

- **Pixie**: pixel art and sprite animation with GIF and sprite sheet export.
- **Loft**: lo-fi beat maker with WAV loop export.
- **Memeforge**: memes, caption bars, stickers and deep fry.
- **Wallsmith**: procedural wallpapers in six styles at any resolution.
- **ASCII Cam**: webcam as ASCII, Matrix, Game Boy, pixel art and halftone, with recording.
- **Nook**: pomodoro timer with generated ambient sounds, streak heatmap and mini window.
- **Pulse**: music visualizer with six styles and video recording.
- **Fontwall**: preview text in every installed font.
- **Boop**: 16-pad keyboard soundboard with built-in sounds and mic recording.
- **Chatmock**: chat screenshot maker in three looks.
- **Namewell**: name generator in ten categories.
- **Keyrace**: typing test with code mode, ghost racing and history graph.
