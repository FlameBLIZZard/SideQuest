## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

First release with twelve apps and a launcher:

- **Pixie**: pixel art and sprite animation with GIF and sprite sheet export.
- **Loft**: lo-fi beat maker with WAV loop export.
- **Memeforge**: memes, caption bars, stickers and deep fry.
- **Wallsmith**: procedural wallpapers in six styles at any resolution.
- **ASCII Cam**: webcam as ASCII, Matrix, Game Boy, pixel art and halftone, with recording.
- **Nook**: pomodoro timer with generated ambient sounds, streak heatmap and mini window.
- **Pulse**: music visualizer with six styles and video recording.
- **Fontwall**: preview text in every installed font.
- **Boop**: 16-pad keyboard soundboard with built-in sounds and mic recording.
- **Chatmock**: chat screenshot maker in three looks.
- **Namewell**: name generator in ten categories.
- **Keyrace**: typing test with code mode, ghost racing and history graph.
